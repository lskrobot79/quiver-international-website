About

Building stronger communities through holistic development

We believe that lasting change happens when people are empowered to grow spiritually, physically, economically, and socially — and that it endures only when communities own it.

Quiver International is a nonprofit organization dedicated to equipping individuals, families, and communities worldwide. We believe that lasting change happens when people are empowered to grow spiritually, physically, economically, and socially.

Through our comprehensive programs, we provide education, training, wellness initiatives, and community development resources to underserved populations. Our approach is holistic — addressing the whole person and the whole community.

We work alongside local partners to create sustainable impact that transforms lives and builds thriving communities.

Programs

Eight ways we equip

Comprehensive initiatives for community transformation — each one built with local partners and designed to keep running after we step back.

01

Baseball Development

Youth baseball training that builds character, teamwork, and athletic skill. We use America's pastime to teach discipline, perseverance, and leadership while providing equipment and coaching to underserved communities.

→ 02

Women's Functional Fitness

Empowering women through functional fitness that builds strength, confidence, and community — practical movement that enhances daily life and creates supportive networks of women encouraging one another.

→ 03

Educational Media & Content

Developing and distributing podcasts, training videos, online courses, and written materials on leadership, business, and personal development.

→ 04

Leadership & Business Training

Business development workshops, leadership skills training, organizational capacity building, and sustainability training for individuals and organizations.

→ 05

Health & Wellness

Community health through fitness and wellness coaching, nutrition education, and vocational training in food preparation and healthy cooking.

→ 06

Literacy & Language Development

Language preservation and literacy through translation projects, literacy program development, and educational materials for underserved language communities.

→ 07

Technology Services

Technology infrastructure and support for other charitable organizations — website development, database solutions, and technology training.

→ 08

Family & Community Development

Holistic family and community development through parenting education, community programs, and spiritual and personal development resources.

→

Our Impact

Our name reflects our mission — to fill communities with equipped, empowered people who can make a positive impact on the world around them.

We believe every person has the potential to create change. Through education, training, and support, we help unlock that potential and build stronger families and communities.

Creating lasting change means measuring what endures: skills that stay, leaders who remain, and programs a community can carry on its own.
